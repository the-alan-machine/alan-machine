# About the Papers

The chapters tell the story. The papers make the argument.

Some chapters have a companion paper with the full scientific treatment of their subject: the
derivations, the assumptions and the references a specialist would expect. Each paper stands on its
own, with its own authors, abstract and references, and can be cited independently of the book. A
chapter can lean on several papers, and a paper can support several chapters.

No papers have been written yet.
