# Constants


Values from the 2019 revision of the SI, where the constant is exact by definition, and from
CODATA otherwise.

| Constant | Symbol | Value | Unit | Standard uncertainty | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed of light in vacuum | $c$ | $299792458$ | $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ | exact | SI (2019), exact by definition |
| Planck constant | $h$ | $6.62607015 \times 10^{-34}$ | $\mathrm{J\,s}$ | exact | SI (2019), exact by definition |
| Reduced Planck constant | $\hbar$ | $1.054571817 \times 10^{-34}$ | $\mathrm{J\,s}$ | exact | h / 2 pi, exact; value truncated to 10 significant digits |
| Boltzmann constant | $k_B$ | $1.380649 \times 10^{-23}$ | $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ | exact | SI (2019), exact by definition |
| Newtonian constant of gravitation | $G$ | $6.67430 \times 10^{-11}$ | $\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ | $1.5 \times 10^{-15}$ | CODATA 2018 |
