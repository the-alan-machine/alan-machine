# Contributing


This book is written in the open, and anyone can improve it. You do not need to know git, and you
do not need to be a physicist: many of the most useful contributions are a single error found by a
careful reader.

## What you can contribute

- **An error**: a typo, a broken link, or a wrong equation or number.
- **A challenge**: a claim you think is wrong, unsupported, or classified as the wrong kind. The
  book marks every claim as established, reported, an extrapolation, speculation or philosophy, and
  a wrong label is an error too.
- **A reference**: a paper or book that should be cited, or listed as further reading.
- **New numbers** for a Building Alan living review, with their unit, date and source.
- **New text**: a chapter, a section, an interlude, a living review or a paper, proposed before it
  is written.
- **A translation**, or a correction to one.
- **An idea** that is not a proposal yet ("what if Alan could...").

## Without git: a form on GitHub

You need only a free GitHub account. Each form asks for what a reviewer needs; write in English.

| Form | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Erratum](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=erratum.yml) | A typo, a broken link, or an error in an equation or a number |
| [Scientific challenge](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=scientific-challenge.yml) | A claim that is wrong, unsupported, or classified as the wrong kind |
| [Content proposal](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=content-proposal.yml) | A new chapter, section, interlude, living review or paper |
| [Living review update](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=living-review-update.yml) | New or corrected numbers for a Building Alan living review |
| [Reference suggestion](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=reference-suggestion.yml) | A paper or book that should be cited or listed as further reading |
| [Translation](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/issues/new?template=translation.yml) | A wrong or missing translation, or an offer to translate |

Ideas and questions go to
[Discussions](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/discussions). The people who
look after the part of the book you name are told about your issue, and they answer it there.

## With a pull request

To change the text yourself:

1. Open "Edit this page" at the foot of a page for a small fix in the browser, or fork the
   [repository](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine) and work on your own copy.
2. Make the change, keeping the book's [style guide](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/blob/main/STYLE.md).
3. Open a pull request and fill in its template.
4. A person who looks after those pages reviews it, and a maintainer merges it.

The title of the pull request, the sign-off every commit needs, the checks to run and the
step-by-step setup are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md),
which has the complete rules. Translators start from
[docs/translating.md](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/blob/main/docs/translating.md).

## Who looks after the book

Every part of the book has moderators, and pages can have curators who keep them accurate. They
are listed in [Moderators and Curators](https://the-alan-machine.github.io/the-alan-machine/appendices/people.html#sec-people), with the maintainers of each translation. How to take one of these roles
is in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/the-alan-machine/the-alan-machine/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
