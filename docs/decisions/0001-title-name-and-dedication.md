# 0001. Title, machine name and dedication

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status: accepted

## Context

The project started as "Alan Computer". The name honors two people: Alan Turing and Alan Ricardo,
the author's brother.

## Decision

- The book is titled **The Alan Machine**.
- The machine is called **Alan** in running text and **the Alan Machine** when it is introduced.
  "Machine" echoes the Turing machine: in his 1936 paper, Turing called his machines
  "a-machines", for automatic machines.
- The dedication names both Alan Turing and Alan Ricardo. Its wording is the author's.
- The repository is named after the machine, `alan-machine`, so a future change of title does not
  change the URL.

## Consequences

- The homage lives in three places: the machine's name, the title and the dedication.
- The premise also echoes Isaac Asimov's short story "The Last Question" (1956), in which a
  computer is asked, across the ages, whether entropy can be reversed. The book may cite and
  paraphrase the story but must not quote it at length, because it is still under copyright.
