# 0002. Licenses and Developer Certificate of Origin

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status: accepted

## Context

Without a license, all rights are reserved, and nobody can legally reuse the book or contribute to
it.

## Decision

- Text, figures and all other non-code content: **CC BY 4.0**.
- Code, such as the code that generates figures and simulations: **MIT**.
- Contributors certify their right to contribute with the **Developer Certificate of Origin**
  (`git commit -s`), not with a contributor license agreement. Once the repository is on GitHub,
  the DCO app enforces the sign-off on every pull request.

## Consequences

- Anyone can share and adapt the book, including commercially, as long as they credit it.
- Contributors keep the copyright on their work and sign nothing beyond the sign-off line.
- The same split (CC BY 4.0 for content, MIT for code) is used by The Turing Way, a community-written
  open book, which shows it works for collaborative books.
- `LICENSE` holds the CC BY 4.0 notice and links to the legal code. The full legal code can replace
  it without any change in meaning.
